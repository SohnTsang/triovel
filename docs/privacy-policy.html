Triovel Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use Triovel, we collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials (via Apple Sign-In or email/password).
  • Trip data: Trip titles, dates, timezone preferences, and currency settings you create.
  • Content you share: Text posts, photos, and videos you upload to shared trips.
  • Financial data: Bill amounts, currencies, payer information, and payment records you enter for expense splitting.
  • Location text: Optional location descriptions you type for trip blocks (we do not access GPS or device location).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Triovel trip collaboration experience:

  • Display your trips, posts, and expenses to you and your trip members.
  • Sync data across devices so your trip is always up to date.
  • Calculate expense balances between trip members.
  • Store and deliver photos/videos you share with your trip group.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose beyond trip collaboration.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is shared only with:

  • Your trip members: Shared posts, bills, and payments are visible to members of the same trip.
  • Infrastructure providers: We use Supabase (database, authentication, storage) and PowerSync (data synchronization) to operate the service. These providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.

Private posts (marked "Just Me") are never visible to other users and are not synced to other devices.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption in transit).
  • Data is stored in Supabase-managed PostgreSQL databases with row-level security policies.
  • Photos and videos are stored in Supabase Storage with access controls.
  • Local data is stored on your device using SQLite (via PowerSync) for offline access.
  • Authentication tokens are managed by the Supabase Auth SDK with automatic refresh.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and authentication records are permanently deleted.
  • Your membership in all trips is removed.
  • Posts and bills you created may be anonymized to preserve trip integrity for other members.
  • Photos and videos you uploaded are deleted from cloud storage.
  • Local data on your device is cleared immediately.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data: View all your trips, posts, and expenses within the app.
  • Delete your account: Use the "Delete Account" option in Settings to permanently remove your account and associated data.
  • Export your data: Contact us to request a copy of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Triovel is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of Triovel after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
