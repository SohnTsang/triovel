Privacy Policy

Effective March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Triovel ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Triovel mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you create an account and use Triovel, you voluntarily provide us with certain personal information, including:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and authentication credentials. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the information you authorize Apple to share, which may include your name and a relay email address.
  • Trip content: Trip titles, dates, display timezone preferences, currency settings, block titles, location descriptions, and any text, photographs, or videos you create or upload within the Service.
  • Financial information: Bill amounts, currencies, designated payers, expense split configurations, and payment records you enter for the purpose of group expense tracking. Triovel does not process actual monetary transactions.

Information collected automatically

We collect limited technical information necessary for the operation and security of the Service:

  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and language settings for the purpose of providing a localized and compatible experience.
  • Usage data: Aggregate, non-identifying metrics such as the number of trips created, blocks and posts created, and media upload success rates. These metrics are stored locally on your device and are used solely for beta quality assessment. We do not track individual user behavior, browsing patterns, or create behavioral profiles.

Information we do not collect

Triovel does not collect precise geolocation data, does not access your device's GPS, does not read your contacts or calendar, and does not use any advertising identifiers or tracking technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes and no others:

  • Providing the Service: To display your trips, posts, and expenses; to synchronize data across your devices; and to enable collaboration with your trip members.
  • Calculating balances: To compute and display shared expense balances between trip members based on the bills and payments you enter.
  • Authentication and security: To verify your identity, maintain your session, and protect against unauthorized access to your account.
  • Service improvement: To analyze aggregate usage patterns during the beta period in order to identify bugs, improve performance, and prioritize feature development. This analysis uses aggregate metrics only and does not involve profiling individual users.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, marketing to third parties, automated decision-making, or profiling.

How We Share Your Information

With your trip members

When you create or contribute to a shared trip, other members of that trip can see your shared posts, photographs, videos, bills, and payments. Content you designate as private ("Just Me") is never transmitted to other users' devices, never stored in a manner accessible to other users, and never displayed to anyone other than you.

With service providers

We use the following third-party services to operate Triovel. Each processes data on our behalf pursuant to data processing agreements:

  • Supabase: Provides database hosting, user authentication, and file storage infrastructure. Data is stored in managed PostgreSQL databases with row-level security policies. Supabase's infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services.
  • PowerSync: Provides real-time data synchronization between our server database and the local SQLite database on your device, enabling offline functionality.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple): If you choose to authenticate via Apple Sign-In, Apple processes your authentication according to Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to any third party for any purpose, including advertising or data brokerage.

Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS with TLS.
  • Server-side data is protected by PostgreSQL row-level security policies that enforce access control at the database level, ensuring users can only access data associated with trips they are members of.
  • Photographs and videos are stored in access-controlled storage buckets with per-user authorization.
  • Local data on your device is stored in a SQLite database managed by the PowerSync SDK. This data remains on your device and is accessible only through the application.
  • Authentication tokens are managed by the Supabase SDK with automatic token refresh and are stored securely on your device.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide the Service. When you delete your account:

  • Your user profile and authentication records are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our authentication system.
  • Your membership records in all trips are deleted.
  • Photographs and videos you uploaded are permanently deleted from cloud storage.
  • Posts and bills you created within shared trips may be anonymized (your name replaced with a generic identifier) rather than deleted, in order to preserve the integrity of shared trip records for other members. This ensures that expense calculations and trip history remain consistent for your fellow travelers.
  • All locally cached data on your device, including downloaded images and synchronization data, is cleared immediately upon account deletion.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can view all of your trip data, posts, bills, and payments directly within the application at any time.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and associated data through the "Delete Account" option in the application's Settings screen. Account deletion is processed immediately.
  • Data portability: You may contact us to request an export of your personal data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Correction: You can update your display name and profile information within the application.
  • Withdrawal of consent: If you signed in with Apple, you can revoke Triovel's access through your Apple ID settings at any time.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States, where our infrastructure providers maintain their servers. We rely on our service providers' compliance frameworks and data processing agreements to ensure adequate protection of your data during such transfers.

Children's Privacy

Triovel is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under the applicable minimum age, we will take prompt steps to delete that information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the application or via the email address associated with your account prior to the changes taking effect. Your continued use of the Service following notification of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

privacy@triovel.app

我們不會出於任何目的（包括廣告或資料仲介）向任何第三方出售、出租、租賃或交易您的個人資訊。

資料儲存與安全

我們實施合理的技術和組織措施來保護您的個人資訊：

  • 裝置與伺服器之間傳輸的所有資料均使用 HTTPS 搭配 TLS 進行加密。
  • 伺服器端資料受 PostgreSQL 行級安全策略保護，在資料庫層級強制執行存取控制。
  • 照片和影片儲存在具有使用者級授權的存取控制儲存桶中。

資料保留

只要您的帳戶保持有效且提供服務所需，我們即保留您的個人資訊。當您刪除帳戶時：

  • 您的使用者個人資料和認證紀錄將從我們的認證系統中永久且不可逆地刪除。
  • 您在所有旅行中的成員紀錄將被刪除。
  • 您上傳的照片和影片將從雲端儲存中永久刪除。
  • 您在共享旅行中建立的貼文和帳單可能會被匿名化而非刪除，以保持其他成員共享旅行紀錄的完整性。

本隐私政策说明 Triovel（以下称"我们"）在您使用 Triovel 移动应用程序及相关服务（以下统称"本服务"）时，如何收集、使用、存储及共享信息。访问或使用本服务即表示您已阅读并理解本隐私政策。

我们收集的信息

您直接提供的信息

当您创建账户并使用 Triovel 时，您自愿提供以下个人信息：

  • 账户信息：您的姓名、电子邮件地址及认证凭证。若您使用 Apple 登录，我们会收到您授权 Apple 共享的信息，可能包括您的姓名及转发电子邮件地址。
  • 旅行内容：旅行标题、日期、显示时区偏好设置、货币设置、区块标题、地点描述，以及您在本服务内创建或上传的文字、照片或视频。
  • 财务信息：您为群组费用追踪目的而输入的账单金额、货币、指定付款人、费用分摊设置及付款记录。Triovel 不处理实际的金钱交易。

自动收集的信息

我们收集本服务运营及安全所需的有限技术信息：

  • 设备信息：设备类型、操作系统版本、应用版本及语言设置，用于提供本地化且兼容的体验。
  • 使用数据：汇总的非识别指标，如创建的旅行数量、区块和帖子数量、媒体上传成功率。这些指标存储在您的设备上，仅用于测试版质量评估。我们不追踪个人用户行为、浏览模式，也不创建行为档案。

我们不收集的信息

Triovel 不收集精确位置数据、不访问您设备的 GPS、不读取您的联系人或日历、不使用任何广告标识符或跟踪技术。

我们如何使用您的信息

我们仅将收集的信息用于以下目的：

  • 提供服务：显示您的旅行、帖子和费用；跨设备同步数据；以及与旅行成员进行协作。
  • 计算余额：根据您输入的账单和付款计算并显示旅行成员间的共享费用余额。
  • 认证与安全：验证您的身份、维持您的会话、保护您的账户免受未经授权的访问。
  • 服务改善：在测试版期间分析汇总使用模式以识别错误、改善性能及排定功能开发优先级。此分析仅使用汇总指标，不涉及个人用户分析。

我们不会将您的个人信息用于广告、向第三方营销、自动化决策或分析。

我们如何共享您的信息

与旅行成员共享

当您创建或参与共享旅行时，该旅行的其他成员可以看到您的共享帖子、照片、视频、账单和付款。您指定为私人的内容（"只有自己"）永远不会传输到其他用户的设备，也不会以其他用户可访问的方式存储，更不会向您以外的任何人显示。

与服务提供商共享

我们使用以下第三方服务来运营 Triovel。每个服务商均根据数据处理协议代表我们处理数据：

  • Supabase：提供数据库托管、用户认证及文件存储基础设施。数据存储在具有行级安全策略的受管 PostgreSQL 数据库中。
  • PowerSync：在服务器数据库与设备上的本地 SQLite 数据库之间提供实时数据同步，实现离线功能。
  • Apple（使用 Apple 登录）：若您选择通过 Apple 登录进行认证，Apple 会依照 Apple 自身的隐私政策处理您的认证。

数据存储与安全

我们实施合理的技术和组织措施来保护您的个人信息：

  • 设备与服务器之间传输的所有数据均使用 HTTPS 搭配 TLS 进行加密。
  • 服务器端数据受 PostgreSQL 行级安全策略保护，在数据库层级强制执行访问控制。
  • 照片和视频存储在具有用户级授权的访问控制存储桶中。
  • 设备上的本地数据存储在 PowerSync SDK 管理的 SQLite 数据库中。
